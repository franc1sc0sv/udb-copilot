# /udb-figures

Run `udb figures <SUBJECT> [--source SRC-...] [--dpi 220]`. PNGs land in
`vault/<SUBJECT>/figures/<ID>/` with an `index.json`; that folder may be opened with Read.
Look at the figure before solving: never assume angles, heights or force directions.
A figure is interpreted, not quoted.
