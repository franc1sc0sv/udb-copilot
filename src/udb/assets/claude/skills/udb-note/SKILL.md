# /udb-note

Launch the `scribe` subagent. One note, one idea, every section the subject kind requires
(`udb note new <SUBJECT> <term>` writes the template). Every claim with its anchor from
`vault_quote`. At least one mermaid diagram. Finish with `udb verify --path <file>`.
